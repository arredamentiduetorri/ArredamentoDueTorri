This notice explains how Arredamenti Due Torri s.n.c. processes the personal data of people who visit this site or fill in the contact form, under Article 13 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

## Data controller

Arredamenti Due Torri s.n.c., based in Via Magellano n° 3, 40017 San Giovanni in Persiceto (BO), Italy. For any request about how your data is processed, you can write to arredamentiduetorri@libero.it.

## Data collected and purpose

When you fill in the form on the Contact page, we collect your full name, email address and the message you send us. This data is used only to reply to you and handle your request for information or a quote; it is not used for marketing purposes and is not shared with third parties for commercial reasons.

## Legal basis

Processing is based on the consent you give by submitting the form and, when the request concerns a quote, on the performance of pre-contractual steps requested by you.

## Data retention

Messages received through the contact form are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and afterwards for the period required by tax and administrative law.

## Third-party services

The contact form is handled through the hosting service that runs this site, which receives and forwards messages by email. The map on the Contact page loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which may involve a connection to their service.

## Your rights

At any time you can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, by writing to arredamentiduetorri@libero.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
